## Description

Read your script. Don't memorize it.

CueCam is a calm, easy-on-the-eyes teleprompter that helps you speak with confidence,
whether you're filming a video or giving a speech. Read on a warm, book-like screen, or
record yourself while your words scroll right over the live camera. Your script stays on
screen to guide you, but it never appears in the video.

WHY CUECAM
- Looks like a reader, not a gadget. Warm, low-contrast themes that are kind to your eyes.
- Record-ready. The teleprompter floats over the camera so you keep eye contact with the lens.
- Everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no subscription.

MADE FOR
- Creators filming TikToks, Reels, YouTube videos, and talking-head content
- Speakers, presenters, and anyone reading vows, a toast, or a pitch
- Teachers, coaches, and course creators recording lessons

FEATURES
- Screen mode: read on a full-screen, distraction-free prompter
- Camera mode: scroll over the live camera and record, then save to Photos or share to TikTok and Instagram
- Voice-follow: the script moves as you speak, using on-device speech recognition
- Karaoke highlight: the line you're reading stays bright while the rest dims
- Hands-free volume control: play, pause, or record with the volume buttons
- Six reader themes (light and dark), seven fonts, custom text color, adjustable size and reading position
- Aspect guides for 9:16, 1:1, and 16:9 so you frame perfectly for every platform
- 3-2-1 countdown, mirror mode for beam-splitter rigs, and smooth, steady scrolling
- Starter templates: YouTube intro, TikTok hook, best man speech, wedding vows, sales pitch, product demo
- Write or paste any script, and share it as a PDF

FREE TO START
Use CueCam free with up to three scripts, the Paper theme, and core reading and recording.
Unlock CueCam Pro once (no subscription) for unlimited scripts, every theme and font,
custom colors, voice-follow, karaoke highlight, mirror mode, and hands-free control.

Your words, your pace, your story. Just press play.
